Research

Research at the interface of geomechanics, sustainable materials, and AI.

My work aims to make computational civil engineering models more efficient, interpretable, and useful for resilient infrastructure design.

Soil-Structure Interaction

Dynamic interaction problems, pile uplift capacity, elastodynamic Green's functions, and multilayered media.

Scientific Machine Learning

Physics-informed neural networks, Fourier Neural Operators, explainable AI, and model-agnostic interpretation.

Sustainable Materials

Stabilized soil, UHPC, fiber-reinforced concrete, SCMs, zeolite, borosilicate glass powder, and road materials.

Sep 2025 - Current

Physics-Informed Neural Network for Elastodynamics Green's Function

Advisor: Chair Professor Ernian Pan, NYCU

Developing a PINN framework to evaluate elastodynamic Green's functions and provide efficient computational solutions for dynamic soil-structure interaction problems.

Sep 2025 - Current

Advanced Neural Operator Modeling for Multilayered Isotropic Media

Advisor: Chair Professor Ernian Pan, NYCU

Applying complex-valued residual networks and Fourier Neural Operators to model wave propagation and elastodynamic behavior in isotropic half-space multilayered media.

Jan 2026 - Current

Physics-Guided Machine Learning for Pile Uplift Capacity Prediction

Advisor: Chair Professor Ernian Pan, NYCU

Designing interpretable, physics-guided learning methods to capture mechanical behavior in soil-pile interaction and predict pile foundation uplift capacity.

Jan 2026 - Jun 2026

Explainable AI for Stabilized Soil

Advisor: Chair Professor Ernian Pan, NYCU

Developed multiple machine learning models and multilayered explainability techniques for lime- and cement-stabilized soil strength prediction.

2023 - 2025

Sustainable Materials and Civil Engineering AI

Aligarh Muslim University and collaborating institutions

Conducted projects on UHPC strength prediction, fiber-reinforced concrete, SCM-blended lightweight concrete, borosilicate glass powder, abaca fiber, zeolite, road safety, and soil stabilization.
