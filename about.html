About Me

A researcher building toward a PhD in geotechnical engineering.

My academic path brings together structural and material engineering, computational modelling, experimental materials research, and machine learning for civil infrastructure.

Biography

Academic Profile

I am a Master of Science student in Structural and Material Engineering at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taiwan. My current research is advised by Chair Professor Ernian Pan and focuses on computational methods for elastodynamics, multilayered media, and soil-structure interaction.

Before joining NYCU, I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Civil Engineering at Aligarh Muslim University, India, where I ranked in the top 10 of my class and developed a strong foundation in structural materials, experimental testing, and applied machine learning for civil engineering problems.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in Geotechnical Engineering and related fields. I am especially interested in research groups working at the intersection of geomechanics, offshore infrastructure, semi-analytical modelling, sustainable materials, and interpretable AI.
